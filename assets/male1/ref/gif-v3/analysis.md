## Handoff constraints and integration suggestions (not observed facts)
All generated sheets drift slightly in face position/scale/hair. Individual reference-tile files are merely crops, NOT registered sprites, NOT transparent mouth or eyelid patches. NO verified aligned feature patches are claimed. Do not swap whole tiles into the live avatar. Cloud must align/redraw eyelids and lips to neutral master and create clean plates/alpha layers.
Keep ONE continuous mouth contour. At closed state, lips meet; as aperture opens retain left/right corner correspondence and move upper/lower contour smoothly. Clip interior/teeth/tongue within the same aperture. Do not leave old smile line while adding another opening below. Rounded A/O versus broadened smile need width+height+corner changes, not only vertical scaling of a box. Teeth must follow upper lip, not a permanent giant white grin decal. Blink upper lash contour must descend/redraw, hide original open-eye lashes/iris correctly; neutral closed and happy closed arcs differ. Half-blink requires additional manual drawing because generated top-right corrective state is too weak. Mouth row1col2 barely differs from closed; manually prepare true near-open intermediary to avoid popping.
